# Senior combined A4 PDF

After Check Point 6 Update 1 (source commit 2d952ab040357515009ed7299da95d9462ef01e6), the PDF trial combines premiums and benefits in one row per attained age. It applies to both 99/10 and 90/90.

Portrait A4 is retained. The seven columns are age, main annual premium, combined rider annual premium, total annual premium, death benefit, accidental death benefit, and maturity. All schedule rows are retained, including the maturity-only row. Body row spacing adapts to the entry age with an 8.5-point body font for supported data. The three-line health note remains conditional on selecting fixed-benefit health coverage. Excel retains its existing separate sheets; calculator values are unchanged.

The prior two-table PDF is recoverable from TYINSURE_Check_Point_6_Update_1.zip.
